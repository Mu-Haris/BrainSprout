Frequently asked questions

How do I change the sound?

Use the music and lesson audio sliders in the app's Settings (tap the Settings button on the Home screen). Music quiets automatically during videos and spoken feedback. Set either slider to zero to mute that audio, or switch background music off.

I already purchased Premium. How do I get it back?

In Settings, choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for your subscription. A pending Ask to Buy request unlocks Premium after it is approved. Check your internet connection if the App Store cannot be reached.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Choose Manage Subscription in the app's Settings, or open your iPhone or iPad Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting BrainSprout or resetting progress does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

How does Coloring work?

Choose a paint, then tap a white area. Match every area to the reference picture. Undo reverses the last paint action and Reset clears the current picture. The activity moves on after a correct match.

How does Spelling work?

Tap a scrambled letter to fill the next space, or drag it into a space. Tap a placed letter to send it back to the tray. Correct words move on automatically.

How do I reset learning progress?

In Settings, choose Reset Progress. It clears lesson positions, completion flags, and learning results on this device. Audio preferences and Premium purchases are kept.

Which devices are supported?

BrainSprout runs on iPhone and iPad.
