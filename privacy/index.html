Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the BrainSprout app ("BrainSprout", "the app") by Kinex Apps ("we", "us") handles information. BrainSprout is an educational app for learners and families.

In short: We (Kinex Apps) do not collect or store any personal information on our servers. There are no accounts and no analytics. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may be personalized if you allow it; Premium removes all ads.

Your learning stays on this device

BrainSprout stores lesson progress, activity results, and sound preferences locally on your device. Coloring pages are temporary activities; the app does not upload drawings or offer a public gallery. Device data may be included in your device backups according to your Apple settings.

No accounts

BrainSprout does not require an account. The app itself does not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or camera images, and does not include third-party analytics.

Advertising in the free version

The free version of BrainSprout shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banner, full-screen, app-open and native ads). Google may collect and use the following to show, personalize and measure ads and to prevent fraud:

  • the device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when BrainSprout shows Apple's tracking permission prompt;
  • device type and model, operating-system and app version, and approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • ad impressions, taps and related diagnostics.

Your choices: if you decline Apple's tracking prompt, ads are not personalized using the IDFA. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form appears before any ad loads, and you can change your choice later in BrainSprout's Settings under Privacy choices.

This information is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We (Kinex Apps) do not receive it in a form that identifies you.

Tapping an ad may open Safari or the App Store outside BrainSprout. A Premium subscription removes all ads.

Purchases through Apple

Apple handles subscriptions and payment details. BrainSprout uses Apple's StoreKit to receive verified purchase and subscription information so it can unlock Premium. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple's services are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Audio and learning content

Learning pictures and videos are bundled with the app. Spoken word feedback uses the device's built-in speech synthesis. The app does not record or transmit your voice.

Children's privacy

BrainSprout is a general-audience educational app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). A parent or guardian should supervise young learners, decide whether to allow ad tracking, and may choose Premium to remove all ads. If you believe a child's information has been shared with us in error, contact us and we will delete it.

Settings and purchases

Settings, purchases and in-app help open directly. Please supervise your child and keep purchases protected with Screen Time or Ask to Buy in your Apple Account settings.

Your choices

Parents can adjust sound, turn off haptics, or reset saved learning progress in the app's Settings. Removing the app removes its local data from the device, subject to your backup settings. Resetting progress or deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions through your Apple Account.

Emails you send us

If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use them only to answer you and do not share them. Please never send your child's personal information or your payment details.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change, and change the "Last updated" date above.
